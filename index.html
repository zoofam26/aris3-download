Install

Three steps and you are in

Download the APK

Tap the download button above. The APK comes from this page's GitHub releases — no mirrors, no repackaging.

Allow the install

Android will ask for permission to install from your browser ("install unknown apps") the first time. Allow it once and continue.

Sign in with ARIS

Use the same username and password as the aris3.udsm.ac.tz portal. The app keeps them in your device's secure storage only.

FAQ

Good questions, straight answers

Is Aris3 an official UDSM app?

No. Aris3 is an independent, unofficial client built by a UDSM student for students. It signs into the same ARIS 3 portal you already use and never modifies university data — it reads and, where the portal allows, fills forms on your behalf. For official communication always refer to university channels.

Where do my credentials go?

Your username and password are stored only in your device's hardware-backed secure storage (Android Keystore) and are sent exclusively to aris3.udsm.ac.tz when you sign in. There are no third-party servers, no analytics SDKs and no tracking of any kind.

The app says my password expired — what now?

ARIS 3 expires passwords every 90 days. Change your password on the portal website (aris3.udsm.ac.tz), then sign in with the new one in the app. The app walks you through exactly what happened instead of showing a blank error.

What happens when a new version is released?

The app checks this page's version manifest. When a new release is published you are asked to update; older builds pause until they are updated so that everyone stays on a compatible, verified build.

iOS, Windows or macOS?

They are built and being tested; they will be published here as soon as they pass the same live verification the Android build goes through. Android is shipped first because it is what most students carry.
